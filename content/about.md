# About AgroMaître

## Our Mission

AgroMaître was founded to bridge the gap between traditional farming and modern technology. We believe every farmer — from a small family operation to a large agribusiness — deserves access to enterprise-grade precision agriculture tools.

## Our Vision

A world where every field, greenhouse, and livestock operation is intelligently monitored, sustainably managed, and economically optimized through the power of AI, IoT, and data analytics.

## Our Values

### Security First
ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II compliant. Your agricultural data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

### Real-time Intelligence
From soil moisture to satellite imagery, decisions happen in milliseconds.

### Sustainability
Every optimization reduces water waste, chemical use, and carbon footprint.

### Farmer-Centric
Built with agronomists, designed for the field — not a boardroom.

## Compliance & Security

AgroMaître is built to meet the highest security standards:
- **ISO 27001**: Information security management
- **SOC 2 Type II**: Security, availability, and confidentiality controls
- **GDPR**: Full compliance for European data protection
- **End-to-end encryption**: All data encrypted with AES-256
