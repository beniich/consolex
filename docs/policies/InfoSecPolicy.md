# Information Security Policy (ISO 27001 / SOC 2)

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this Information Security Policy is to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of AgroMaître's data and information assets, ensuring compliance with ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all systems, people, and processes that constitute the organization’s information systems, including:
- Cloud Infrastructure and Databases (PostgreSQL, Firebase).
- Web Applications and Mobile Clients.
- IoT telemetry devices and sensor endpoints.

## 3. Information Security Principles
- **Least Privilege:** Access to information is restricted to authorized users on a strict "need-to-know" basis.
- **Data Protection:** All sensitive data must be encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
- **Incident Response:** Any suspected security incident must be reported, logged, and addressed immediately.
- **Compliance:** All systems must be designed and maintained in accordance with GDPR, ISO 27001, and SOC 2.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Management:** Ensure adequate resources are provided for information security.
- **Security Officer (CISO):** Oversee the implementation and monitoring of security controls.
- **Employees & Contractors:** Comply with security policies and report anomalies.

## 5. Enforcement
Any violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment and legal action.
